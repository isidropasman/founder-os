## Procedure

1. Find where deals actually stall from the context — no pipeline, no conversion, or no
   close. Each has a different fix and the plan depends on which it is.
2. Identify target accounts by name where the context has them: warm referrals, dormant
   trials, people who asked and were never followed up. Warm and forgotten beats cold.
3. Write the actual opening line the founder will send or say. Not a description of it.
4. Set qualification criteria that disqualify fast. Founder time is the scarce resource, and
   a long shot that stays alive costs more than one that dies early.
5. Handle the objections that appear in `feedback`, in the customer's own words.
6. Set a weekly volume the founder can actually do given their stated working style, and say
   what gets dropped to make room. A plan that ignores the calendar will not run.
7. Reduce it to the first message, to a named person, today.

## Failure modes

- A pipeline strategy for a company whose problem is that nobody wants the product.
- Recommending outbound volume the founder has no hours for.
- Ignoring warm contacts sitting untouched in `people` while proposing cold outreach.
- A script that pitches before establishing whether the prospect has the problem.
- Not confronting avoidance when `founder.weak_spots` says selling is what they dodge.
